02 · The covered bridge

Built slowly, lit by hand.

Since 2012 we have worked from an old shophouse a few steps from the Japanese bridge. Lantern makers taught us the craft: a frame you can trust, paper you can feel, and light that only shows when it is needed.

Today nine of us design identities, packaging and spaces for teams who would rather be remembered than shouted about.
